# ADR-0011: Show a numbered episode name as sent, gated by air date

TMDB returns names of the form `Episode 7` in two unrelated situations, and no field in the response marks which is which. It is a stand-in for a scripted episode TMDB has no title for yet — Grey's Anatomy S23E1 read `Episode 1` before broadcast — and it is also the real, permanent name for entire genres, because soaps, news programmes, reality shows and daily serials do not title their episodes at all. The two are separable by deduction rather than by a flag: the show's genre tells them apart directly, and so does the age of the episode, since a stand-in is replaced within days of broadcast while a permanent name never is. Only the age signal is used here, because the season response carries no genre field and reading one would cost a second request per show.

Two treatments were tried and both were wrong. Suppressing the name left the card's title line empty, which collapses it and makes the card a line shorter than its neighbours; worse, it treated a permanent name as "not resolved yet", so the lookup re-checked such shows every six hours for the life of the application process. Replacing it with a spelled-out `Season 2 - Episode 7` filled the line but invented a label that merely restates the code printed directly beneath it.

Sampling `/tv/on_the_air` (60 shows, checked 2026-08-14) settled it. Every title still numbered more than two days after broadcast belonged to a show that will never have episode titles — `Secret Lives` at `Episode 4978`, `Jornal Nacional`, `60 Minutes`, `Chibi Maruko-chan`, `Gran hermano`. Scripted drama carried its real title within two days without exception: Silo at one day, Reacher at two, Ted Lasso at three, all named. The stand-in case therefore barely survives broadcast, and the Watch Next page only ever lists episodes that have already aired.

So the name is shown exactly as TMDB sends it — for a soap that is the only sensible label, and for everything else it is correct by the time the episode reaches the page — and air date decides whether it might still improve. A numbered name counts as provisional only within `_PLACEHOLDER_WINDOW` days of broadcast, and is taken as final after that, which bounds the whole thing at eight re-checks per episode against the six-hour floor. A missing or unparseable air date counts as recent, since re-checking a few times is cheaper than freezing a stand-in in place forever.
